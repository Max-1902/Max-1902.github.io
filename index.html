No. 02

World of Data

A live economic and geopolitical dashboard with forecasts computed in the browser and an AI analyst that answers by calling 25 live-data tools (World Bank, EIA, ACLED, market quotes), so it explains measured numbers instead of inventing them. Each model was chosen by a rolling-origin backtest across 217 economies (1990–2025) with a rule written down before the run: AR(2) won on GDP but lost on inflation and unemployment, which now ship a no-change forecast. IMF forecasts still beat AR(2) in 142 of 185 economies. Weekly US gas-storage forecasts beat the five-year seasonal norm by 5% over 561 weeks (Diebold–Mariano p = 0.004).

8% / 20%
Lower GDP error than “no change”, one and three years ahead
93–96%
Of 2010–2025 outcomes inside 95% intervals calibrated on pre-2010 forecasts

TypeScript · React · World Bank / EIA / ACLED APIs · LLM tool use

No. 03

NEOTracker

An iOS app that tracks asteroids passing Earth from live NASA near-Earth-object data, with 3D orbit views, an impact calculator, space news and guides. Held up to the sky, it shows what's directly overhead: planets, stars, galaxies and asteroids, with names, photos and distances. A GPS-based night-sky planner computes planet positions and rise/set times with Meeus/VSOP87 algorithms.

Swift · SwiftUI · SceneKit · NASA APIs
