02

World of Data

A live economic and geopolitical dashboard with forecasts computed in the browser and an AI analyst that answers by calling 27 live-data tools (World Bank, EIA, ACLED, market quotes), so it explains measured numbers instead of inventing them. Each model was chosen by a rolling-origin backtest across 217 economies (1990–2025) with a rule written down before the run: AR(2) won on GDP but lost on inflation and unemployment, which now ship a no-change forecast. IMF forecasts still beat AR(2) in 142 of 185 economies. Weekly US gas-storage forecasts beat the five-year seasonal norm by 5% over 563 weeks (Diebold–Mariano p = 0.004).
