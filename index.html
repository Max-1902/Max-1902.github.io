About

I’m a Computer Science Master’s student at DePaul University, focused on applied AI for finance.

My curiosity for programming and systems started during my Bachelor’s, where the question I kept coming back to was how systems actually work and how I could use them in finance. That led me to DePaul, where I’m pursuing a Master’s in Computer Science with a focus on AI engineering, computational finance, and systems.

One example of what I build is a fully local RAG system with an evaluation harness over SEC 10-K filings.

At DekaBank, in Structuring & Derivatives Trading, I developed a regex-based algorithm that classifies news articles by issuer, category and country, which is currently being migrated into production. I also built an interactive world map prototype for the internal trading dashboard, presented it to senior engineers, and prepared internal wiki documentation on AI-assisted and agent-based coding workflows.

I speak German, English, and Polish.

No. 01

Local RAG & Evaluation Pipeline

A fully local RAG pipeline over FY2025–26 10-K filings of 5 companies (Microsoft, NVIDIA, Tesla, Lockheed Martin, BlackRock), using hybrid dense + BM25 retrieval with no external API calls. Oracle tests showed generation, not retrieval, was the bottleneck.

No. 02

Macroeconomic Forecasting System

A macro dashboard on 50+ World Bank/OWID series per country with in-browser AR(2) GDP forecasts (1,000+ tests). Backtested against a naive baseline across 24 countries: AR cut error 21–25% for Poland, Turkey and Indonesia but lost for China, Japan and Korea, where the full-sample fit pulls forecasts toward boom-era growth.
